Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-19

Rebuild is a self-guided app for getting through a breakup, divorce, loss or other hard change. What you share here is personal, so we collect as little as we can, keep most of it only on your phone, and never sell it or use it for advertising.

Who we are

Rebuild is made by Beglar, an individual developer, who is the controller of your personal data under this policy ("we", "us"). You can reach us at supportatrebuild@gmail.com.

What stays only on your phone

These are saved on your device and are never sent to our servers:

  • Your journal entries.
  • Your mood check-ins and which sessions you have finished.
  • Your answers from onboarding: what you are going through, when it happened, what you need, and whether you have children.
  • Important dates you add, and your reminder and display settings.

If you delete the app, or delete your account in the app, this information is removed from the phone. We cannot see it or recover it.

What we collect when you create an account

An account is optional for the free parts of Rebuild and needed for Rebuild Premium, so a purchase stays with you on any phone.

  • Sign-in details: your email address and, if you use Apple or Google, the account identifier they give us. If you sign in with Apple, you can choose to hide your email; we then receive a private relay address.
  • Your name, only if Apple or Google shares it. We use it only to greet you in the app. It is never spoken in recordings.
  • Purchase status: whether your account has Rebuild Premium, which plan, and when it renews or ends. Payment itself is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.

What is collected automatically

  • When the app downloads new sessions, artwork or app updates, or asks for a Premium recording, our servers and those of our providers receive your device's IP address and basic request details (such as the time and the app version), as part of normal server logs.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in (performing our contract with you).
  • To unlock Rebuild Premium and restore it on a new phone (performing our contract with you).
  • To deliver content and keep the service working and safe (our legitimate interest in running the app).
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we do not sell or share it for those purposes.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase (accounts, database and file storage), hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • RevenueCat (managing subscriptions and purchases), based in the United States. It receives your account identifier and purchase records from Apple or Google.
  • Apple and Google, for Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google and in-app purchases, under their own privacy policies.
  • Expo (app updates delivered over the internet), based in the United States.

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area or the UK, it is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your account and the data linked to it from our database straight away. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept as their own legal and tax obligations require. Server logs are kept for a short time (typically up to 30 days).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: You > Delete account. This deletes your account and its data on our servers and wipes what Rebuild saved on that phone. If you signed in with Apple, it also removes Rebuild from the apps using your Apple ID. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or under Californian law), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. Write to supportatrebuild@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Rebuild is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. The question about whether you have children is used only on your phone to suggest parenting sessions, and we never collect information about your children.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with providers that use industry-standard security. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what we hold to a minimum.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
